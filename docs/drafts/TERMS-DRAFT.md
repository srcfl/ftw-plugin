# FTW plugin terms

These terms cover the FTW plugin for Grok Bot, Cursor and other AI assistants that read Agent
Plugins, and its connection through FTW Cloud. Sourceful Labs AB (reg. no. 559382-0458), Kalmar,
Sweden, provides the integration.

## What the plugin provides

The plugin offers FTW setup guidance and, for an authorized home, tools to explain readings, plans
and equipment health. It is free, takes no payments and offers no purchases. Your assistant account,
a suitable FTW host and any equipment or network services you use have their own requirements and
terms.

## Your home and your approval

Connect only a home you own or have permission to use. Review the rights and expiry before approving
an assistant in FTW. You can end its access in FTW. Reading does not grant control. Short actions
stay within the approved limits; requests for lasting goal changes wait for the owner's approval.

## Check readings and advice

FTW and this integration are in beta. Readings can be old or missing, plans can change and an
assistant can make mistakes. Check the source, time and limits before acting. A plan, an accepted
command or an estimate does not prove physical response or savings. Sourceful does not promise a
particular saving or an uninterrupted beta service.

Keep the equipment's own protections in place. Use a qualified electrician for mains wiring, meters,
fuses and other work that requires one. Setup guidance does not replace the manufacturer's
instructions or a required professional check.

## Data and access

The privacy notice explains what Sourceful handles, who receives it, how long it is held and how to
exercise your rights. FTW Cloud can read assistant requests and box answers while passing them on.
The company that runs your assistant (for example xAI for Grok Bot, or Anysphere for Cursor)
processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy and your account terms. Ending FTW
access prevents further authorized reads; it does not delete earlier conversations held by the
assistant.

Use only the rights needed for your task. Do not bypass another home's access checks, extract
secrets or disable equipment protections.
